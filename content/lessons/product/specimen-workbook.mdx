## 2. Business objective

**Objective:** Earn demand from PMs who need technical depth, taught on a live product: free PM for trust and showcase, free tech as the wedge, packaging only if demand earns it.

**One-page case (filled):**

```text
Diagnosis:          PM↔tech gap. Slide-deck PM courses and eng blogs rarely teach
                    stack literacy on a live product the reader can see.

Objective link:     Earn tech-for-PMs demand; free PM builds trust; packaging TBD.

Target market:      Professionals crossing the PM↔engineering boundary.
                    English-first for v1.

Beachhead:          PMs without eng depth who need tech literacy (primary).
                    Engineers learning PM = pin two on the same home page.

Personas:           User: PM who owns roadmap but cannot judge timelines /
                    eng reviews / AI-drafted code.
                    Buyer (later): same person; v1 free; packaging waits on demand.

Job / problem:      Enough stack literacy for reviews, timelines, and small-team
                    MVPs, without becoming an engineer.

Bet:                Free PM spine + free tech wedge on one portfolio; paid depth
                    only after demand.

Hypothesis seeds:   (1) Beachhead PMs start free tech on this product.
                    (2) Outreach brings non-friend waitlist / tech starters.
                    (3) Free PM spine still builds trust without being the whole bet.

Primary metric:     Tech-lesson starts + waitlist from non-friends (demand);
                    PM Lesson 1→2 as trust/activation guardrail.

Guardrails:         Lesson quality; no paywall on free lessons; dual scoreboard
                    (product demand vs personal teach/showcase) stays honest.

GTM wedge:          Public URL + LinkedIn / PM communities / meetups; PLG.

Ask:                Founder time for PM + first tech lessons; thin waitlist;
                    hosting at MVP scale; no paid GTM in v1.

90-day learning:    Do non-friend PMs join the waitlist / start free tech?
                    Does the PM spine still earn completion as trust?
```

## 4. Experiments

### Card 1: interviews (problem)

1. **We believe** PMs without eng depth
2. **will** name engineering literacy / eng-review fear as a top issue
3. **because** they cannot approximate timelines or challenge estimates without a model of the stack
4. **Right when** in 12 interviews across beginner / growth / generalist PMs (not technical PMs), at least 8 unprompted top-three issues include eng communication, stack literacy, or timeline judgment
5. **Cheapest test:** 30-minute problem interviews; do not open with “would you pay?”
6. **If wrong:** beachhead pain is elsewhere; revisit strategy before a tech curriculum

### Card 2: waitlist (demand before curriculum)

1. **We believe** beachhead PMs who hit Learn
2. **will** open the eng track and join the waitlist
3. **because** they want stack literacy for reviews, timelines, MVPs/POCs
4. **Right when** within 30 days, enough non-friend waitlist emails (example bar: 40) and follow-up reply ≥25%
5. **Cheapest test:** honest “Join the waitlist” CTA; optional WTP band around ~$50; no Stripe; no fake eng Lesson 1
6. **If wrong:** keep free PM; do not invent a tech dump or a price from vanity clicks

### Card 3: free tech wedge (after signal)

1. **We believe** waitlist / beachhead PMs
2. **will** start and often finish a free tech lesson on this site
3. **because** literacy on the live product beats slide-deck eng courses
4. **Right when** within 30 days of publish, named N non-friend starters and a finish-rate bar
5. **Cheapest test:** ship 1–3 free tech lessons after Card 2 earns signal
6. **If wrong:** free tech on this product is not the wedge; reposition before twelve eng lessons

## 6. Product leadership

No fake VP org. Duties still apply on a founder-PM build.

| Duty | How it shows up here |
|---|---|
| Clear uncertainty | Waitlist before the tech curriculum; interviews before twelve eng lessons |
| Take blame | Weak lesson copy or a confused home is on the author, not “the stack” |
| Distribute rewards | Cite books and practitioners; credit community feedback in the open |
| Vision | Tech-for-PMs beachhead; free PM as trust; packaging only if demand earns it |
| Purpose | Readers who need stack literacy for reviews and timelines, not vanity traffic |

**Escalation when someone pulls Stripe into Now:**

1. Scoreboard this quarter is waitlist / tech interest, not revenue.
2. Strategy refused Stripe until a funded packaging hypothesis.
3. That ask goes to Later until Card 2/3 clear.
4. If you want packaging Now, bring a kill rule and a demand number, not a checkout mock.

## 8. Analytics and GTM

| Primary | Non-friend waitlist / tech starts |
| Guardrails | Lesson quality; no paywall on free spine |
| Tools | Lightweight events + spreadsheet or simple analytics |
| GTM read | Which community post brought activated readers |

**Tracking plan (waitlist join):**

| Event | Properties | Owner | Where |
|---|---|---|---|
| `waitlist_join` | `channel`, `utm_*`, `friends` (bool) | PM defines; eng implements | Client form submit + server store |
| `waitlist_view` | `referrer`, `utm_*` | Eng | Client page view on `/waitlist` |
| `lesson_start` | `slug`, `track`, `friends` | PM + eng | Client when lesson body mounts |
| `lesson_finish` | `slug`, `track` | PM + eng | Client (scroll/complete heuristic) or explicit CTA |
| `home_track_click` | `track` (`pm` / `eng`) | Eng | Client on home CTAs |

Pageviews alone are not the scoreboard.

## 9. Pricing

**Offer (hypothesis, not live Stripe yet):** paid product-engineering depth, **$49 one-time**, ~**10 years** access. Free PM spine stays free. Monthly refused for this knowledge offer.

| Line | Assumption |
|---|---|
| Hosting + domain + tools | ~$20/mo → ~$2,400 / 10 years |
| Fees + support reserve | ~$5 variable per buyer |
| Contribution | ~$44 |
| Break-even | ~**55 buyers** |
| Demand sketch | ~400 non-friend waitlist × 15% convert ≈ 60 buyers |

**Kill:** if non-friend WTP is near zero, keep free teaching; do not invent a price from vanity clicks. Update dollars when invoices are real.

## 10. Product sense and AI

**Decision we actually faced:** ship an honest tech waitlist before free tech lessons, or ship eng Lesson 1 to look complete.

| Line | Filled |
|---|---|
| Scoreboard | Non-friend waitlist joins (demand before curriculum) |
| Leaning | Waitlist first: Card 2 before Card 3 |
| What would change my mind | Interview synthesis shows beachhead already activated without a door; or waitlist sits dead while friends beg for Lesson 1 and qualitative says the door is the blocker |
| AI’s job | Challenge the leaning (edge cases, copy, instrumentation), not replace the kill rule |

Rubber-stamping “just ship the lessons, AI can write them fast” fails the scoreboard.

## 11. Vision, brand, and Figma

**One-page brief (this site):**

- **Beachhead:** PMs without eng depth who need stack literacy
- **Job:** learn PM method and (later) tech on the live product
- **Feeling:** lab notebook: serious, calm, two-way (PM and eng)
- **Refuse:** purple “AI SaaS” chrome; bootcamp landing energy; corporate navy deck; split chrome for “PM track vs Eng track”
- **Constraints:** readable first; open pieces then tweak (Fumadocs, Poppins for Latin + Devanagari); public Learn without login in v1
- **Scoreboard:** waitlist / tech starts; PM Lesson 1→2 as trust

**User flow: join tech waitlist:**

1. Land home → see eng track CTA  
2. Open `/waitlist`  
3. Decide: join or leave  
4. Submit email (success)  
5. Confirmation / next step  

**Failure branch:** form error or bounce → stay on waitlist with clear retry; do not fake “lesson unlocked.”

**Tokens (working):**

| Token | Value |
|---|---|
| `bg` | Paper / off-white site surface |
| `text` | Charcoal / zinc ink |
| `accent` | Restrained (not rainbow SaaS purple) |

| Space | Comfortable docs rhythm (readable line length) |
